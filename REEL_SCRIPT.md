# Your Life in Dots: Video Reel Scripts

Two complete scripts matched directly to the application demo mode timing.
Neither version uses emojis, em dashes, or gloomy phrasing.

## Version 1: Vertical Reel (30 Seconds)

Format: 9:16 vertical video for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.

- On-Screen Text: What does your entire life look like on one screen?

- Voiceover (optional): What does your whole life look like on a single screen?

- On-Screen Text: Every dot is one week.
- Visual: Tap dot touches Day (12), Month (Jun), and Year (2004), then selects 80 years and taps Show my life.
- Voiceover (optional): Pick your birth year and picture a life of eighty years.

### 0:05 to 0:08: The Canvas Appears
- On-Screen Text: 4,160 weeks.
- Visual: The empty grid wave glides down from top to bottom, revealing 80 rows of grey dots.
- Voiceover (optional): Here is your canvas. Four thousand one hundred and sixty weeks.

### 0:08 to 0:15: The Fill
- On-Screen Text: Your journey so far.
- Visual: Emerald, teal, and cyan dots fill in reading order, racing through the decades as the counter climbs to 1,166 weeks.
- Voiceover (optional): Every week you have lived fills in. School days, college, first jobs, late night drives.

### 0:15 to 0:18: You Are Here
- On-Screen Text: You are right here.
- Visual: Week 19 of age 22 pulses with an emerald ring and a tag reading "You are here".
- Voiceover (optional): This pulsing dot is this exact week.

### 0:18 to 0:24: The Turn
- On-Screen Text: 3,008 Sundays ahead.
- Visual: The headline turns to: "You have about 3,008 Sundays ahead." Every grey dot stands ready.
- Voiceover (optional): Look at what is still in front of you. Over three thousand Sundays ahead.

### 0:24 to 0:28: Milestones Ahead
- On-Screen Text: 713 full moons. 58 Pongal mornings. 15 World Cups.
- Visual: Smooth scroll down through the stat cards. Numbers count into place.
- Voiceover (optional): Hundreds of full moons, festivals, and memories waiting to be made.

### 0:28 to 0:30: Closing Call to Action
- On-Screen Text: Map your dots at wrench-wise.com. Built by Wrench Wise.
- Visual: Closing card with "Share my dots" and the quiet Wrench Wise credit.
- Voiceover (optional): See your life in dots. Built with code by Wrench Wise.

## Version 2: Horizontal Video (60 Seconds)

Format: 16:9 widescreen video for YouTube, LinkedIn, and website embedding.

### 0:00 to 0:02: The Hook
- On-Screen Text: Most people measure life in years. Try measuring it in weeks.
- Visual: Desktop two-column layout at 1440 by 900. Clean light theme with soft emerald tones.
- Voiceover (optional): Most of us think about life in years. But what happens when you look at it in weeks?

### 0:02 to 0:07: Setting the Stage
- On-Screen Text: One dot equals one week.
- Visual: Tap dot selects a birthday: 12 June 2004. Highlights the eighty year horizon pill.
- Voiceover (optional): In this app, one dot equals one week. Eighty rows of fifty-two dots. A complete life on one screen.

### 0:07 to 0:11: Generating the Map
- On-Screen Text: Pressing "Show my life".
- Visual: The user taps the primary button. The card dissolves and the grid fades in.
- Voiceover (optional): Press "Show my life" and watch what happens.

### 0:11 to 0:16: The Empty Grid Wave
- On-Screen Text: 4,160 weeks on a single screen.
- Visual: Smooth wave of quiet grey dots flows down the left column. Decade markers 0, 10, 20 through 70 line the gutter.
- Voiceover (optional): The full grid appears. Every single week has a dedicated place.

### 0:16 to 0:26: The Lived Journey
- On-Screen Text: 1,166 weeks lived.
- Visual: Colored dots cascade from top left to bottom right. Emerald shifts smoothly to teal, then bright cyan. The live headline counter counts from 0 to 1,166 in sync.
- Voiceover (optional): The color rushes in. Twenty-two years of memories, lessons, and growth. That is one thousand one hundred and sixty-six weeks already written into your story.

### 0:26 to 0:32: The Present Moment
- On-Screen Text: This week is yours.
- Visual: Close zoom onto the current dot. It expands to 1.8 times its size and returns, settling into a gentle two-second pulsing ring. The label reads: "You are here".
- Voiceover (optional): Here is this week. Right here in the present. It pulses quietly, reminding you where you stand today.

### 0:32 to 0:42: Perspective Shift
- On-Screen Text: You have about 3,008 Sundays ahead.
- Visual: The headline smoothly transforms. The subline clarifies: "Every grey dot is a week still ahead of you."
- Voiceover (optional): And then comes the real perspective shift. Look at the grey dots ahead. You have about three thousand and eight Sundays still waiting for you.

### 0:42 to 0:52: Meaningful Numbers
- On-Screen Text: Beyond the calendar.
- Visual: The camera shifts to the stat cards on the right. Numbers count up smoothly: 713 full moons ahead, 58 Pongal mornings, 15 cricket World Cups, and 847 million heartbeats so far.
- Voiceover (optional): That is more than seven hundred full moon nights, dozens of harvest mornings, and over eight hundred million heartbeats recorded so far.

### 0:52 to 0:56: Interactive Exploration
- On-Screen Text: Hover any dot to travel through time.
- Visual: Cursor hovers a dot in decade 10: "Age 12, week 31. Around Oct 2016." Then hovers a future dot: "Age 45, week 10. Around Aug 2049."
- Voiceover (optional): You can hover any dot on the canvas to see the exact age and season.

### 0:56 to 1:00: Closing Call to Action
- On-Screen Text: Spend a few of them learning something new. Built by Wrench Wise.
- Visual: The closing card displays: "Share my dots" and "Make yours at wrench-wise.com".
- Voiceover (optional): That is a lot of Sundays. Spend a few of them learning something new. Built with code by Wrench Wise.
